# Reveal surface brief

- Scope and mode: `/reveal`; an Experience-to-Read bridge between ritual draw and deep interpretation.
- Audience and job: a reader who has just completed a spread needs to see the whole symbolic structure land, reconnect it to the original question, and understand how the positions will be read.
- Primary action: enter deep reading. Secondary action: save or share a privacy-safe spread summary without including the question.
- Content and proof: real `CardImage` artwork, orientation, canonical spread positions, `ReadingContext.question`, and `getSpreadExperience` organization paths.
- Constraints: inherit Midnight tokens and ritual altar geometry; solid surfaces, hairlines, serif reading text, mono notation, square controls; no prototype spread switcher, glass, pills, or changes to ritual animation parameters.
- Chosen direction: a desktop split of semantic midnight altar and independently scrollable manuscript folio. Single, triangle, four-aspect, seven-card, and Celtic-cross spreads retain distinct coordinates; mobile converts the altar into a contained horizontal snap track before the folio.
- Memorable moment: the full spread settles over the etched ritual rings while the folio names each drawn card beside its position meaning.
- Unresolved: a sticky desktop folio action rail is optional future polish, not part of the current contract.
